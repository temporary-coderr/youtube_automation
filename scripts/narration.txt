Ever replay a tiny awkward moment for hours? A strange thing you said. A pause that lasted one second. A look you cannot stop thinking about. Your brain is scanning for social threats, trying to understand what happened and how to avoid it next time. But replaying the memory does not mean the moment is still dangerous. The loop feels urgent because your attention keeps returning to it. Try this: name the thought without arguing with it. Ask whether there is anything useful to learn. If there is, take the lesson. If not, let the memory pass and return to what is happening now. You are not that one awkward moment. You are the person living beyond it. This is WISE-LAB. Understand your mind. Change how you move through the world.
